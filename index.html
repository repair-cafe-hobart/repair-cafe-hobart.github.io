Good things, kept going

A community that knows how to mend.

Repair Café Hobart is a community get-together where people bring repairable household items and work alongside volunteer fixers. It’s a place to share practical skills, ask questions and make connections.

We believe repair is a useful, hopeful way to reduce waste and help everyday objects last longer. Everyone is welcome to come along, whether you’re bringing an item, lending a hand or simply curious.

How a Repair Café works

Bring your curiosity. We’ll work it out together.

01⌕

Tell us about it

Check in with the welcome team and describe what has happened to your item.

02⚒

Explore a repair

A volunteer will look at it with you. You’re encouraged to stay and take part as we investigate.

03☕

Share the moment

Have a cuppa, meet people and pick up a tip or two, whether or not a repair is possible today.

ⓘ Repairs are offered in a shared community setting. Some items may be unsafe, too complex, or need parts we don’t have, so a repair can’t be guaranteed.

Volunteer-run, with a donation welcome

Repair Café Hobart is run entirely by volunteers. We ask for a donation to help cover our expenses. We say, “If you can get it through the door, we can take a look”—though cracked screens, smart TVs and watches are unlikely to be repairable here.

Before bringing an item, please email repaircafehobart@gmail.com or message us on Facebook. Tell us the problem and symptoms, what you have already tried, and, for appliances, the make, model and approximate age. Include a photo if it may help a repairer identify the problem.

We’ll ask our repairers what they think and get back to you.

Bring it along

What can you bring?

Volunteer skills vary at each session. Tell us what you have and we’ll do our best to point you in the right direction.

⌁

Everyday household

Small items, appliances and useful things that might be repairable.

⚙

Tools & technology

There may be help with bikes, tech, sharpening and mechanical items.

✂

Clothing & craft

Ask about sewing, patching, jewellery, leatherwork and other handcrafts.

A few helpful things to know

  • Bring only items you can safely carry and stay with during the session.
  • Include cables, chargers, keys or other parts that belong with the item.
  • If you can, bring any instructions or information about the fault.
  • Contact us first about large, hazardous or unusual items.

The repair categories above reflect volunteer roles in the roster workbook. Availability at each café needs to be confirmed.
