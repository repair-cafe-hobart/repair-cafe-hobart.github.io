Good things, kept going

A community that knows how to mend.

Repair Café Hobart is a community get-together where people bring repairable household items and work alongside volunteer fixers. It’s a place to share practical skills, ask questions and make connections.

We believe repair is a useful, hopeful way to reduce waste and help everyday objects last longer. Everyone is welcome to come along, whether you’re bringing an item, lending a hand or simply curious.

We are part of a worldwide Repair Café network.

What can you bring?

Volunteer skills vary at each session. Tell us what you have and we’ll do our best to point you in the right direction.

Everyday household

Small items, appliances and useful things that might be repairable.

⚙

Tools & technology

✂

Clothing & craft

Ask about sewing, patching, jewellery, leatherwork and other handcrafts.

Is your item unusual or complex?

We say, “If you can get it through the door, we can take a look”; however, it is unlikely we will be able to repair cracked screens, smart TVs and watches.

If you have an unusual item that you think might be complex to repair, please email repaircafehobart@gmail.com or message us on Facebook. Tell us the problem and symptoms, what you have already tried, and, for appliances, the make, model and approximate age. Include a photo if it may help a repairer identify the problem.

We’ll ask our repairers what they think and get back to you.

A few helpful things to know

  • Bring only items you can safely carry and stay with during the session.
  • Include cables, chargers, batteries, keys or other parts that belong with the item.
  • If you can, bring any instructions or information about the fault.
  • Contact us first about large or hazardous items.
  • For Linux upgrades, allow at least 40 minutes, as the process takes time.
  • For computer and related technology repairs, where possible, back up your documents, photos, emails and other files before attending.
